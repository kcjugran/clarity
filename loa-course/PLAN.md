## Process

1. Course overview — all 10 lessons at a high level, so the through-line is clear.
2. Lesson by lesson: Karan gives the description + transcript; Claude reads both,
   asks for any image/diagram it needs, and drafts the chapter.
3. Once all 10 chapters are settled, assemble the book.

## Conventions

- Raw transcripts land in `source/` as `lesson-NN-transcript.md` (committed so
  nothing is lost when the session container is recycled).
- Images/screenshots land in `img/`.
- Concept diagrams are drawn as original inline SVG in the Clarity house style
  rather than screenshotted from the slides where possible.
